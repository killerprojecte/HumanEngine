## Experimental implementation versus validated benefit

The graph, semantic maintenance and policy paths must be executable and source-linked; they are not completed by adding switches or diagrams. Their experimental switches can remain default OFF until evidence supports activation. Offline tests can prove bounded work, contracts, deletion/authorization behavior and mechanical held-out criteria, but cannot establish real-model semantic extraction accuracy or sustained task improvement. Neither frequency, graph centrality, a model's confidence nor a mechanical receipt is independent proof of truth or benefit.

The implementation is intentionally not an arbitrary ontology, nickname-based identity resolver, unrestricted program synthesizer or autonomous external-action agent. Predicates and action patterns remain registered, typed and limited by existing permission controls. This bounds the engineering without silently substituting a read-only shadow for the approved learning/retrieval loop.
